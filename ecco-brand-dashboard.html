Retention curve over 90 days
Percentage of customers in each cohort still actively subscribed at each day. Ecco cohort enrolled in 90-day program; control cohort declined enrollment.
Methodology

Cohort definition. All customers who subscribed to Mars Men T-Boost between Mar 1 – May 1, 2026 were eligible. The Ecco cohort (n=847) accepted the program invitation; the control cohort (n=412) declined.

Retention definition. A customer is "retained" if their subscription remained active on the specified day (not paused, not canceled).

Statistical test. Cox proportional hazards model, controlling for subscription tier, product variant, and acquisition channel.

Confidence. p<.001 across all measurement points. 95% CI: 2.4× – 3.2× retention lift.
